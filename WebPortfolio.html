Best Work Gallery - My favorite work from the year.

Recycling Poster

For this assignment I made a poster based on being creative and repurposing things. I included it into my portfolio because it shows my great design skills like creating good color palettes and making one coherent piece.

Personal Logo

For this assignment I designed a seed packet for a “macaron plant.” I choose to include this piece because it’s a good representation of my design skills like, color palette, typography, and overall style. I used a mix of symmetry for the background, and used asymmetry to make the center stand out. I also used a technique of only coloring the macarons to make it stand out.

seed Design Challenge

For this assignment I designed a logo for myself, using things like a record, because I like music, and a compass rose to represent my love for geography. I choose to include this piece because I am very proud of this design. I took elements of my personality to create a good looking design and I think that's a good representation of my design skills.

About Me
